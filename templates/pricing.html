{% if tab == 'markups' %}

Sell price = base × (1 + markup). Enter the markup as a percent: 40 means the customer pays 1.40× the base price. Glass and spacer (including sealant) markups are separate. These values apply to new calculations immediately. Tier 5 is the Tier 2 price times the formula multiplier (2 unless that setting is changed), including the temper add-on, so Tier 5 is exactly twice Tier 2. Editing Tier 2 updates that Tier 2 price. On every other tier the temper add-on stays the flat tempering amount. The extra per sq ft, the irregular shape surcharge, and the DWG drawing creation fee stay flat and are not marked up by tier. Quotes and orders already saved keep the prices they were given.

{% elif tab == 'currency' %}

A customer's country chooses the quote and order currency. Customer users cannot change it. Ora Glass staff can price a quote in either currency, and must convert it before it is confirmed for a customer whose country uses the other one. CAD prices on new quotes use this ERP rate. Quotes and orders already saved keep the rate stored on them.

The rate in effect today is {{ '%.4f'|format(current_rate) }} CAD per 1 USD, from {{ current_effective }}.

ERP exchange rate

{% elif tab == 'glass' %}

Each glass row is seeded from the pricing workbook. Base used is the price the sell formula multiplies: raw price × (1 + tariff %) × (1 + waste %). Saving a row recomputes that base only when the raw price, tariff, or waste changed. In price = no keeps the row out of new calculations. Deactivate hides it as well. The second copy of a duplicated workbook name stays out of the price. Quotes and orders already saved keep their prices.

{% elif tab == 'temper' %}

One add-on per thickness, in $ per sq ft. It is added when a lite is tempered, it is not marked up, and it is the same at every tier. The seeded amounts are the workbook prices: 4mm 0.50, 5mm 0.60, 6mm 0.65, 8mm 0.95, 10mm 1.00, 12mm 1.20. Quotes and orders already saved keep the temper amount they were calculated with.

{% elif tab == 'components' %}

Spacers use raw $ per foot. Superspacer uses that amount as-is. Technoform multiplies it by the labour add-on (spacer sheet B51, 1.5 = 150%), then averages three sample IGU sizes. That average is the resulting $ per sq ft. Waste is applied after that to get the base the sell price uses. Sealants use price per set. Desiccant, butyl, and corner keys are added only when In price is yes. Quotes and orders already saved keep their prices.
