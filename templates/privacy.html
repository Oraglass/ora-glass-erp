Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Ora Glass handles information in the Ora Glass ERP at oraglass.us. It applies to Ora Glass staff, shop workers, and customer users of the ERP.

Information the ERP stores

The ERP stores the records needed to run Ora Glass's business:

  • Customer accounts, including company details, users, and pricing tiers.
  • Quotes, orders, invoices, credit notes, and payments.
  • Time clock punches and the photos taken on the shop tablet when someone punches.

QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online data is accessed only through Intuit's OAuth connection, and only to sync customers, invoices, payments, and credit notes for Ora Glass's own books. An administrator starts that connection from the ERP. Access and refresh tokens are stored on the server, not in the browser. An administrator can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from the ERP, or from QuickBooks. Disconnecting stops further sync.

Sharing

Ora Glass does not sell this data and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing. The ERP is hosted on Render. QuickBooks data is exchanged with Intuit only for the sync described above.
